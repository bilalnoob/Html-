Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

A simple, traditional Italian pasta dish made with eggs, cheese, cured pork, and black pepper.

Ingredients & Instructions

Ingredients

  • 400g Spaghetti
  • 150g Pancetta or Guanciale
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 50g Pecorino Romano cheese
  • Spices:
    • Freshly ground black pepper
    • Pinch of sea salt

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Crisp the pancetta in a skillet over medium heat until golden brown.
  3. Whisk egg yolks and grated Pecorino Romano together in a small bowl.
  4. Combine pasta with pancetta, remove from heat, and quickly stir in egg mixture to create a creamy sauce.
